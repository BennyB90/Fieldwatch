Privacy Policy

SWEEP is designed so wireless and physical inspection data stays on your device under your control. We operate no user accounts, cloud databases, analytics pipelines, crash-reporting trackers, or advertising SDKs.

1. Offline SWEEP (app.fieldwatch.sweep)

  • No Internet permission: The compiled app.fieldwatch.sweep Android package removes android.permission.INTERNET from its manifest. The app cannot transmit data over the internet.
  • Why Android asks for Location & Nearby permissions: Android requires ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION (while-in-use) and enabled system Location services to return Wi-Fi scan results and unfiltered Bluetooth LE advertisements, plus BLUETOOTH_SCAN, BLUETOOTH_CONNECT, and NEARBY_WIFI_DEVICES on newer Android versions. SWEEP never requests background location (ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION).
  • Why Android asks for Camera permission only during USB-C thermal connection: On Android 9 and newer, Android's USB system requires android.permission.CAMERA before UsbManager will authorize any USB Video Class (UVC) accessory (android.hardware.camera and android.hardware.usb.host are optional). SWEEP never requests Camera permission for wireless scans, uses USB only, and never opens your phone's built-in front or rear cameras. Raw thermal video frames stay in volatile memory only; Saved Spaces store only summary temperature readings (°C median, peak, and ΔT) when you attach a fixture baseline or check.
  • USB wideband spectrum. The tinySA adapter requests access to the selected USB serial device. Traces stay in memory. Saved Spaces retain summaries for bands you choose to attach. Shared PDF, JSON and text logs withhold USB product, manufacturer and serial strings unless you include identifiers. Synthetic previews cannot be saved as measurements.
  • Room scans store no phone GPS: Starting a Room scan, inspecting Saved Spaces, using USB thermal, or connecting a Valentine BLE radar accessory does not request GPS fixes or record your phone's geographic coordinates.
  • Optional Travel location correlation: Travel mode leaves phone GPS off by default. Only if you explicitly check “Also compare my phone’s locations” before starting Travel does SWEEP request foreground GPS fixes to evaluate whether a tracker broadcast was observed at distinct places. You can revoke correlation and erase all saved phone coordinates at any time from the Travel screen.
  • Local retention & backup exclusion: Scan sessions (up to 100 sessions), Saved Spaces (up to 25 spaces and 30 inspection logs per space, plus up to 8 temporary PDF/report shares pruned after 24 hours), Travel observations (up to 8 sessions / 7 days), and battery test readings (up to 12 runs) are stored in app-private storage with Android Auto Backup and device-to-device transfer explicitly disabled across all storage domains.

2. SWEEP + Radio (app.fieldwatch.sweep.radio)

SWEEP + Radio keeps all RF scan history, Travel observations, and reports strictly local on your phone, and adds optional network features that run only after explicit consent:

  • Authorized HTTPS Audio Streams & Rdio Scanner: No stream directory is bundled, and nothing connects until you save an operator-authorized HTTPS URL (or enter an Rdio HTTPS server) and explicitly allow Internet listening. When playing audio, your chosen stream host or Rdio server receives standard HTTPS connection data (such as your IP address and, for Rdio, the listener access code in HTTPS POST bodies, which is never saved to disk). RF scan findings and phone GPS coordinates are never sent to stream hosts.
  • Local Wi-Fi Service Discovery (Network): On Android 13+, tapping Start 60-second discovery and confirming network authorization queries Android's local Wi-Fi DNS-SD API (NsdManager) while the screen remains visible. SWEEP makes no HTTP/TCP connections to discovered endpoints and stores at most 20 local snapshots for up to 7 days.
  • Router Inventory JSON Import: Selecting a JSON file via the Android system document picker parses and validates the selected file locally on your phone without retaining persistent file-access grants or uploading the inventory.
  • Monas SDR. After you authorize the receiver, it receives your IP address, read-only HTTPS requests and optional bearer token. The token and live census are held in memory during the connection. Sharing creates a temporary private report. Each share defaults to aggregate counts and signal statistics. Including address rows, names, company labels or numerical crystal and modulation measurements requires an explicit choice.

3. User-Initiated Sharing & External Apps

  • Reports and inspection PDFs leave SWEEP only when you tap a Share button and choose a destination app in the Android share sheet.
  • By default, shared scan, Saved Space inspection/turnover PDF, USB thermal, Travel, battery, and network exports omit broadcast names, MAC addresses, USB product/manufacturer/serial strings, checklist notes, and coordinates unless you explicitly opt in for that share.
  • Tapping a decoded Remote ID aircraft or operator pin to open a map passes only that broadcast coordinate to your chosen maps app via a standard geo: link.
  • Deleting history inside SWEEP removes local copies immediately, but cannot recall copies you previously sent to another app.

4. Website & Downloads

This static website uses no cookies, browser storage, analytics scripts, or third-party asset hosts. When hosted on Cloudflare Pages, standard edge HTTP request processing by Cloudflare applies solely to deliver static pages and APK files over HTTPS.

5. Open Source & Contact

You can inspect SWEEP's source code, manifest permissions, and automated APK verification checks in the public repository at github.com/BennyB90/Fieldwatch, where questions or privacy issues can also be reported.
