What SWEEP Includes

On-Demand Room Scans

Bounded 20-minute foreground sessions showing matched tracker, camera, and drone indicators alongside unmatched radios, signal history charts, and exact rule match reasons.

Room Scan Comparisons

Compare two completed scans side-by-side to review newly observed, not reobserved, shared, and unresolved addresses alongside radio sample coverage.

Opt-In Travel Mode

Foreground Bluetooth tracker observation (up to 4 hours) with familiar-address exclusions, optional phone GPS correlation (off by default, erasable anytime), and a built-in handset battery meter.

OpenDroneID Evidence

Decodes supported BLE, Wi-Fi beacon vendor IE, and Wi-Fi Aware (NAN) Remote ID frames, keeping aircraft, control-station, takeoff, and fixed operator pins distinct.

254-Signature Catalog

Searchable on-device signature catalog inherited from Fieldwatch, plus a clearly labeled synthetic sample session that never touches your radios or saved history.

Redacted Sharing

Shared session, Travel, battery, and network summaries withhold broadcast names, MAC addresses, and coordinates by default unless you explicitly include them.

Important Capability Limits

  • Not a safe-room guarantee: A quiet scan only means no matching Wi-Fi or Bluetooth broadcasts were received by Android during that window.
  • Invisible devices exist: Offline SD-card audio/video recorders, wired cameras, cellular-only trackers, Bluetooth Classic inquiry devices, and Wi-Fi cameras operating as silent client stations may emit nothing Android scan APIs can observe.
  • Indicators, not verdicts: A matched vendor OUI, service UUID, or repeated tracker sighting is a clue to check visually — never proof of malicious surveillance or unwanted tracking.
  • Signal strength is not distance: RSSI varies with walls, antenna orientation, body shielding, and handset hardware.
  • Alpha status: Current installers are alpha builds undergoing physical handset and RF benchmark validation.

Install & Verify

  1. Download app-sweep.apk (or app-sweepRadio.apk) onto your Android 10+ phone, and optionally verify its SHA-256 digest: 
    # Linux / macOS
sha256sum app-sweep.apk

# Windows PowerShell
Get-FileHash .\app-sweep.apk -Algorithm SHA256
  2. Open the downloaded APK from your phone's Files app and allow installation if prompted. (Google Play Protect may note that alpha sideload builds are not yet registered on Play.)
  3. Open SWEEP and tap Start scan. Android requires Location permission and Location services to deliver Wi-Fi and unfiltered BLE scan results, plus Nearby devices & Notifications on newer Android versions. Room scans never request or store your phone's GPS coordinates.
