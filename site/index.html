What SWEEP Includes

Room Scans & Comparisons

Bounded 20-minute foreground sessions showing matched tracker, camera, and drone indicators alongside unmatched radios, signal charts, and side-by-side scan diffs.

Saved Spaces & Turnover PDFs

Save known-good wireless baselines and expected devices for a room, rental unit, office, or car, run guided physical checklists, and export offline SHA-256 Inspection PDFs.

USB-C Thermal & ΔT Spotlight

Clean-room 16 KB-aligned UVC thermal engine with ΔT Electronics Spotlight, per-fixture Saved Space baselines (+2.0 °C alert threshold), and synthetic preview mode.

Opt-In Travel Mode

Foreground Bluetooth tracker observation (up to 4 hours) with familiar-address exclusions, optional phone GPS correlation (off by default, erasable anytime), and a handset battery meter.

OpenDroneID & 254-Rule Catalog

Decodes supported BLE, Wi-Fi beacon vendor IE, and Wi-Fi Aware (NAN) Remote ID frames with distinct operator pin types, backed by a searchable 254-signature catalog.

Redacted Sharing by Default

Shared session, Saved Space PDF, thermal, Travel, battery, and network exports withhold names, MACs, USB serials, notes, and coordinates unless you explicitly opt in.

Important Capability Limits

  • Not a safe-room guarantee: A quiet scan or completed turnover checklist documents what was checked at that timestamp — it never certifies a space free of surveillance.
  • Invisible devices exist: Offline SD-card audio/video recorders, wired cameras, cellular-only trackers, Bluetooth Classic inquiry devices, and Wi-Fi cameras operating as silent client stations may emit nothing Android scan APIs can observe.
  • Indicators, not verdicts: A matched vendor OUI, service UUID, or repeated tracker sighting is a clue to check visually — never proof of malicious surveillance or unwanted tracking.
  • Thermal shows heat differences, not cameras: Compact ΔT hotspots can come from sunlight, wall chargers, routers, or normal standby electronics; physical USB-C reception on the Topdon TC001 Max awaits hardware bench verification.
  • Signal strength is not distance: RSSI varies with walls, antenna orientation, body shielding, and handset hardware.
  • Alpha status: Current installers are alpha builds undergoing physical handset, USB thermal, and RF benchmark validation.

Install & Verify

  1. Download app-sweep.apk (or app-sweepRadio.apk) onto your Android 10+ phone, and optionally verify its SHA-256 digest: 
    # Linux / macOS
sha256sum app-sweep.apk

# Windows PowerShell
Get-FileHash .\app-sweep.apk -Algorithm SHA256
  2. Open the downloaded APK from your phone's Files app and allow installation if prompted. (Google Play Protect may note that alpha sideload builds are not yet registered on Play.)
  3. Open SWEEP and tap Start scan. Android requires Location permission and Location services to deliver Wi-Fi and unfiltered BLE scan results, plus Nearby devices & Notifications on newer Android versions. Room scans never request or store your phone's GPS coordinates. (Camera permission is requested only if you connect a USB-C UVC thermal accessory in Scan → Thermal; SWEEP uses USB only and never opens built-in phone cameras.)
